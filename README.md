# LA County City Hall Tier List

Rank the city halls of all 88 incorporated cities in Los Angeles County from S to F.

- Deal mode shows one city at a time; tap a tier to place it.
- Drag tiles between tiers (press and hold on phones), or tap a tile and then a tier.
- Rankings save in your browser. "Copy my tier list" copies them as text.

Plain static site: `index.html` + `photos.js`. No build step.

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons; credits and licenses are listed on the page.
39 tiles show the city hall itself. Where Commons had no usable city hall photo,
the tile shows a view of the city and is marked "City view". 8 cities have no photo yet.
