# LA County City Hall Tier List

Rank the city halls of all 88 incorporated cities in Los Angeles County from S to F.

- Deal mode shows one city hall at a time with a swipeable photo strip; tap a tier to place it.
- Drag tiles between tiers (press and hold on phones), or tap a tile and then a tier.
- Tap ⤢ on any tile to flip through all of that city hall's views full screen.
- Rankings save in your browser. "Copy my tier list" copies them as text.

## Photos
- Street-level city hall photos (40 cities, up to 5 each) come from Wikimedia Commons; credits and licenses are listed on the page.
- Every city also gets an aerial view centered on its actual city hall (Esri World Imagery tiles, locations from OpenStreetMap) plus a Google Street View link.
- Cities with no free street-level photo use the aerial view as their tile.

Plain static site: `index.html` + `photos.js`. No build step.
